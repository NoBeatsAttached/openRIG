Build a stage in three moves

  1. Add gear. Drag anything from the Library onto the stage. A preview shows where it lands. Lights snap onto truss and hang upside down on their own.Open Library, tap a piece of gear, then tap the stage where it goes.
  2. Move it. Click something to select it, then drag it. Pull the arrow up or down to change height. Rotate, copy and delete from the bar that pops up.
  3. Check the numbers. Paperwork works out power, rigging loads, the DMX patch and the gear list as you build.

Fastest way to light a truss: select it and hit Add lights.
